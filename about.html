About Us

A spiritual family, a center for discipleship

Central Baptist Church Wakiso is a Christian community committed to glorifying God, proclaiming the Gospel, making disciples, strengthening families and transforming communities through Christ-centered service.

Our Identity

More than a place to gather for worship

We are a spiritual family and a community-based ministry committed to responding to the practical, emotional, educational and spiritual needs of people — particularly children, young people, families and vulnerable members of society.

The church is committed to worship, evangelism, discipleship, compassion, education, mentorship and community transformation.

Ministry Approach

A holistic response to five areas of need

Spiritual

Spiritual Development

Helping people know Christ, understand Scripture and live victorious Christian lives.

Education

Educational Development

Providing opportunities for children and young people to receive quality education.

Emotional

Psychosocial Support

Walking alongside vulnerable young people through difficult circumstances.

Physical

Physical & Social Care

Responding to practical needs — shelter, healthcare, food and clothing.

Leadership

Character Development

Equipping young people and adults with values, skills and leadership capacity.
