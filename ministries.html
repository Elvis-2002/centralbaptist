Ministries

Caring for children, families and communities

Through worship, discipleship, education and outreach, we demonstrate the love of Christ in practical and measurable ways.

Community Outreach

Restoration Home

Restoring Hope. Rebuilding Lives. Creating Futures.

Restoration Home provides holistic care to vulnerable girls, generally from the age of 13 and above, who require protection, support and an environment in which they can grow and develop.

Key areas of support: safe shelter and care, education, healthcare, spiritual support, counseling, mentorship, life-skills development and character formation.

Community Outreach

Kumi Project for Boys

Investing in Boys. Building Responsible Men.

The Kumi Project supports vulnerable boys facing difficult social and family circumstances, helping them overcome barriers and develop into responsible, God-fearing men.

Areas of support: education, healthcare, spiritual development, emotional support, mentorship, character development and counseling.

Community Outreach

Daughters of Zion

Giving Children Hope, Dignity and a Future.

Daughters of Zion supports children whose parents live with physical disabilities and who may face increased vulnerability and limited opportunities.

Areas of support: education, shelter and basic care, spiritual and emotional support, mentorship, counseling and personal development.
