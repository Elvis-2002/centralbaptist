What We Believe

Vision, Mission and Goal

Vision
To be a major and measurable force by creating experiences of inspiration of Jesus Christ that warm the heart and touch the soul.

We desire to see people encounter Christ, experience His transforming love, and grow into who God created them to be.

Mission
To draw people to Christ through Christ-like acts of kindness and service, helping people live victorious daily Christian lives.

Our ministry is both evangelistic and practical — introducing people to Jesus while demonstrating His love.

Goal
To see members of this community believe, change and commit themselves to practicing the righteousness of God.

The ultimate goal of our church is transformed lives, families and communities.
